About Me

I'm Andres Rincon, a CTO in fintech. For more than 20 years I've turned ideas into software: first as a developer, then as an architect, and now leading engineering teams and guiding how organisations adopt generative AI responsibly. I write about architecture design systems, about AI, and about leading people without losing the humanity in the work. I believe complexity should become clarity and that the goal is to be a better version of myself every day. I build bridges, and I teach about Christ, technology, leadership and life, growing through every process. Let's build something together.

To inspire others, break barriers and build bridges that bear witness to excellence — teaching about Christ, Technology, Leadership and Life, while growing through every process and experience.
